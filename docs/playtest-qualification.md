## Shipped gameplay promise and acquisition boundary

The principal shipped behavior is a **server-authoritative, depth-scaled underground encounter Director**: survival/adventure players below the locally calculated surface reserve accumulate pressure, receive warning/telegraph, and encounter bounded, player-targeting mobs from data-defined ecologies. The Director handles locality/participant accounting, spawn-position safety, per-tick/per-second/global caps, native lineage root accounting, and encounter/pressure persistence (`DirectorRuntime`, `DirectorPolicy`, `SpawnLocator`, `DirectorLineages`, `DirectorSavedData`, `DepthDirectorEvents`, `DirectorConfig`; six `src/main/resources/data/better_cave_encounters/director_ecologies/*.json`). Separately, `GeologicalSpawns`/`GeologicalRegistry` integrate tagged underground rock habitats into **native natural** monster spawning rather than creating a second timed spawn loop; `WaterwayDirector`/`WaterwayRegistry` provide bounded exposed-waterway encounters. These are source behavior claims, not evidence of successful gameplay with actual friends.

This repository registers **no player-acquirable item, block, crafting recipe, or GUI**: `DepthDirectorMod` registers event listeners and a common config, and the sole player-facing command `/depthdirector inspect [player]` is permission-level 2 and read-only (`DepthDirectorEvents`). Do not invent an acquisition recipe for the Director. Acquisition/progression checks must instead follow the *full-pack* recipes, loot tables and KubeJS policy. The geological fixture's explicit references are `src/worldGameTest/resources/geological-qualification.json` (`pack_inputs`: `kubejs/server_scripts/policy/global_loot_progression.js`, `kubejs/data/forge/loot_modifiers/global_loot_modifiers.json`, `kubejs/config/crafting_policy.json`, `kubejs/config/quarantined_items.json`, `kubejs/server_scripts/progression/50_malum_soulstone_bootstrap.js`); `GeologicalWorldGameTests.lootContract` checks native drops of `cataclysm:ancient_metal_ingot` and `cataclysm:black_steel_ingot` and removal of forbidden loot sentinels. Those are *referenced external materials*, not items defined or guaranteed obtainable by this mod alone. Geological rock tags come from pack `kubejs/data/better_cave_encounters/**`; the fixture checks the actual registry/tag contract, not any new block registration here.
